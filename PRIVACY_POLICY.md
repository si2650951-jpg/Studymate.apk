## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to provide you with a comprehensive student study management experience:

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Details:** When you register, we collect your name, email address, optional contact number, school/college/institution name, and grade/class.
- **Academic & Timetable Data:** Timetable schedules (days, subjects, timings, room numbers, teacher names), homework tasks (titles, descriptions, due dates, priority, completion statuses), exam schedules (subject, date, syllabus, target score), study goals, milestones, and daily attendance logs.
- **Digital Notes & Attachments:** Subject notes, study summaries, tags, favorites, and pinned items created by you.
- **Study Sessions & Focus Data:** Pomodoro timer duration, subjects studied, session timestamps, and study streaks.
- **Support Inquiries:** Messages, subject lines, feedback, or bug reports submitted via the Help & Support screen.

### B. Information Automatically Collected
- **Device & Usage Information:** Device model, operating system version (Android OS), app version, preferred app theme, language preference, and error/crash diagnostics to ensure application stability.
- **Network State:** Network connectivity status to allow seamless switching between offline storage and cloud synchronization.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for educational and service delivery purposes:
1. **Provide Core App Services:** To organize and display your classes, track homework deadlines, calculate attendance percentages, and record focus study sessions.
2. **Push Reminders & Alerts:** To remind you about upcoming classes, homework submission deadlines, upcoming examinations, and low-attendance warnings.
3. **Account & Cloud Sync:** To securely synchronize your study notes and schedules across your devices via Google Firebase.
4. **App Improvement & Bug Fixing:** To identify application crashes, fix performance bugs, and improve user interface responsiveness.
5. **Customer Support:** To respond to your questions, feedback, or feature requests.

**We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal or academic information to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.**

## 4. Device Permissions We Request

StudyMate requests only the permissions necessary for essential app features:

| Permission | Technical Name | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Internet Access** | `android.permission.INTERNET` | Required to authenticate accounts and sync notes, homework, timetable, and attendance with secure Google Firebase servers. |
| **Network Status** | `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Allows the app to detect internet availability and enable offline caching without disrupting the user experience. |
| **Notifications** | `android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Delivers timely alerts for homework deadlines, class reminders, exam notifications, and attendance threshold warnings (Android 13+). |
| **Vibration** | `android.permission.VIBRATE` | Provides haptic feedback when the Pomodoro focus timer finishes or alert reminders fire. |

*You can revoke notification permissions at any time through your Android device settings.*

## 6. Data Security & Storage

- **Encryption in Transit:** All communications between StudyMate and cloud databases are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2/1.3).
- **Encryption at Rest:** User records and notes stored in cloud databases are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
- **Strict Data Isolation:** Database security rules ensure that each student's personal notes, timetable, and study data are accessible only by the authenticated account holder.
- **Local Offline Cache:** Local copies of your study schedules are protected under standard Android application sandboxing.
