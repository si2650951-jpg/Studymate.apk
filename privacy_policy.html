StudyMate • Privacy Policy

Academic & Study Management Android Application

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 Google Play Compliant Student Safe

1. Introduction

Welcome to StudyMate. We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal and educational data of students, teachers, and users who rely on our study companion platform.

This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding information collection, usage, storage, security, and your data rights in full compliance with Google Play Developer Program policies and global data protection standards.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information needed to help you organize your academic life:

  • Account Profile: Name, email address, optional contact number, school/institution, and grade/class.
  • Academic Management Data: Timetable classes (subjects, timings, room, teachers), homework assignments, exam dates & target scores, study goals, and daily attendance logs.
  • Digital Study Notes: Subject notes, revision cards, summaries, pinned items, and category tags created by you.
  • Focus Study Sessions: Pomodoro timer sessions, focus duration, and study streaks.
  • Support & Feedback: Messages and tickets submitted to our support team.
  • Device Diagnostics: Device model, Android OS version, and non-identifying crash logs to ensure high reliability.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for educational productivity:

  • Organizing your 7-day class schedule, homework tasks, and exam agendas.
  • Calculating your attendance percentage and notifying you if it drops below threshold.
  • Synchronizing your student data securely between sessions using Google Firebase.
  • Delivering timely reminders for upcoming classes, assignment due dates, and study goals.
  • Responding to customer support queries and resolving technical issues.

We do NOT sell, lease, or monetize your personal or student data to third parties, advertising companies, or data brokers.

4. Permissions Used in the App

Permission Technical Identifier Reason Required
Internet Access android.permission.INTERNET Synchronizes student notes, homework, timetable, and study data with Google Firebase cloud servers.
Network State android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Detects online/offline connectivity to smoothly manage local offline caching.
Notifications android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Sends alerts for class timings, assignment deadlines, exam alerts, and low attendance warnings.
Vibration android.permission.VIBRATE Provides haptic feedback upon completion of Pomodoro focus timers.

5. Third-Party Services

StudyMate utilizes secure Google infrastructure for cloud storage and authentication:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: Provides secure student login with email/password and Google Sign-In.
  • Google Firebase Realtime Database / Cloud Firestore: Encrypted cloud database for notes and academic records.
  • Google Play Services: Core application maintenance and system libraries.

6. Data Security & Storage

Your academic privacy is protected with industry-standard security safeguards:

  • All data transmitted across the network is encrypted using TLS 1.2/1.3.
  • Stored cloud data is secured at rest with AES-256 encryption.
  • Student Isolation: Strict database security rules verify that only your authenticated account can access, edit, or view your notes and timetable.

7. Children's & Students' Privacy (COPPA)

StudyMate is an educational tool for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable consent from parents, guardians, or authorized academic institutions. If we learn that personal data of a child under 13 has been collected without consent, we will promptly delete it.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You maintain full control over your study data at all times:

  • You can edit or delete any note, class, exam, or homework item directly in the app.
  • To delete your entire account and all associated data, you can submit a deletion request from the in-app Help & Support screen or email us directly at saidulali66990@gmail.com.
  • Upon verification, your account and all associated cloud data will be permanently wiped within 30 days.
